Rotur provides Turbo-IDE accounts and sign-in. Your Rotur username identifies you on Turbo-IDE, but Rotur manages your credentials and account recovery.

## Mute and block

Open a Turbo-IDE user profile to mute or block that user.

- Muting hides Turbo-IDE notifications from that user. It does not unfollow them on Rotur.
- Blocking stops Turbo-IDE comments and notifications between both users. It does not change either person's Rotur account.

You can review and remove blocks or mutes under **Settings**, then **Safety**.

## Reports and support

Use the report button on a project, profile, or comment when content breaks the community terms. Reports go to Turbo-IDE moderators.

Use the [support page](https://turboshare.org.uk/support) for private account help, safety concerns, legal requests, and moderation appeals. Product bugs belong on the [Roadmap](https://turboshare.org.uk/roadmap), where other users can confirm them and follow their status.

## Export or delete Turbo-IDE data

Open **Settings**, then **Your data** to download a JSON export or delete the data attached to your Turbo-IDE profile.

Deleting Turbo-IDE data signs you out, deletes your Turbo-IDE projects, and removes Turbo-IDE profile data. Turbo-IDE anonymizes public comments when removing them would break a conversation. This does not delete your Rotur account. Signing in with Rotur again creates a fresh Turbo-IDE profile.
